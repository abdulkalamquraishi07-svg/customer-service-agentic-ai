# Order Cancellation Policy

This is a sample policy for a demonstration retail company.

## Orders that have not shipped
Orders in "Processing" status have not left our warehouse and can be cancelled free of charge.
Cancellations are handled by a support representative: ask the support assistant to connect you
with a human representative and give your order number. The full amount is refunded to the
original payment method once the cancellation is confirmed.

## Orders that have shipped
Shipped orders cannot be cancelled. Once the order is delivered you can request a return within
the return window described in the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## Delivered orders
Delivered orders cannot be cancelled; please use the returns process instead.
