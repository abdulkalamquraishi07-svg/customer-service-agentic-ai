# Payments and Billing Policy

This is a sample policy for a demonstration retail company.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI and net banking. All prices are shown in INR and include
taxes.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, do not place the order again. Ask the
support assistant to open a support ticket with the order number. A support representative
checks the payment with our payment provider and, if a duplicate or incorrect charge is
confirmed, refunds the difference to the original payment method.

Never share your full card number, CVV or online banking login details with anyone, including our
support team. We will never ask for them.

## Refund timing
Once a refund is issued, banks usually show it within 5 to 7 business days.

## Price matching
We do not offer price matching with other retailers.
