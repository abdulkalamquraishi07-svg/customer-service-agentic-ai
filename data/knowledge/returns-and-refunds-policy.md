# Returns and Refunds Policy

Applies to orders placed on our online store. This is a sample policy for a demonstration
retail company.

## Return windows
The return window starts on the date the order was delivered.

- Electronics (laptops, tablets, phones, monitors): 10 days from delivery.
- Accessories (headphones, keyboards, cables, cases): 30 days from delivery.
- All other categories: 15 days from delivery.

Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned. Cancelled orders cannot be returned.
Only one return request can be open for an order at a time.

## Refund amount and method
Eligible returns are refunded in full to the original payment method, in the order's currency.

## Approval of large refunds
Refunds above INR 10,000 are reviewed by a senior support representative before they are
processed. A decision is normally made within 48 hours. If no decision is made within 48 hours the
request expires and the customer may submit a new request.

Refunds of INR 10,000 or less are approved automatically once the return is eligible.

## Refund processing time
After approval, the refund is issued to the payment provider immediately. Banks usually show the
refund within 5 to 7 business days.

## Daily refund limit
For security, total refunds per customer are limited per day. Requests above the limit are held
for manual review by the support team.

## How to request a return
Ask our support assistant or visit "My Orders". Please state the order number and the reason for
the return.
