# Warranty and Repairs Policy

This is a sample policy for a demonstration retail company.

## Warranty coverage
Electronics (laptops, tablets, phones, monitors) come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty that
starts on the delivery date. Accessories (headphones, keyboards, cables, cases) come with a
6-month warranty from the delivery date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. It does not
cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or damage from unauthorized repairs.

## How to make a warranty claim
Warranty claims are reviewed by a support representative. Ask the support assistant to open a
support ticket with your order number and a short description of the fault. A representative
contacts you within 2 business days to arrange a repair or replacement.

## Faults within the return window
If a product stops working within its return window, you can choose a return and refund instead
of a warranty repair. See the Returns and Refunds Policy for the return windows.
