# Customer Support FAQ

This is a sample FAQ for a demonstration retail company.

## How do I contact a human representative?
Ask the support assistant to connect you with a person. It creates a support ticket with a
summary of your issue, so you do not have to repeat yourself. Our representatives respond to
escalated tickets within 24 hours.

## How do I make a complaint?
Tell the support assistant what happened and, if it relates to an order, the order number. A
support ticket is created and tracked until it is resolved.

## How do I track my order?
Ask the support assistant for your order status, or use the tracking number with the carrier
shown on your order.

## My payment was charged twice. What should I do?
Do not place the order again. Contact support with the order number; a representative checks
the payment and, if a duplicate charge is confirmed, it is refunded.

## Is my data safe?
Support conversations only use information from your own account. We never share your order
details with other customers.

## Warranty
Electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Warranty claims are
handled by a support representative; ask the assistant to open a ticket.
