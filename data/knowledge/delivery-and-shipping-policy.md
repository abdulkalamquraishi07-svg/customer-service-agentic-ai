# Delivery and Shipping Policy

This is a sample policy for a demonstration retail company.

## Delivery estimates
Every order shows a promised delivery date at checkout. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business
days after the order ships. Orders are shipped with partner carriers such as BlueDart and
Delhivery, and a tracking number is provided once the order ships.

## Delayed deliveries
Delays can happen because of weather, carrier capacity or address issues.

- Up to 2 days past the promised date: please allow a little extra time and track the package
  with the carrier using your tracking number.
- More than 2 days past the promised date: contact support. We open an investigation with the
  carrier and keep you updated.
- More than 7 days past the promised date and still not delivered: you are entitled to a full
  refund or a free replacement. A support representative arranges this with you.

We do not charge for re-delivery attempts caused by carrier delays.

## Orders not yet shipped
Orders in "Processing" have not left our warehouse. Most orders ship within 1 to 2 business days.

## Lost or damaged packages
If tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not receive the package, or it arrived damaged, contact
support within 48 hours of the delivery scan. Include photos for damaged packages.

## Escalation
If you are not satisfied with an answer about your delivery, you can ask to speak with a human
support representative at any time.
